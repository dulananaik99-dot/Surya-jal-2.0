☀️ SURYA JAL

Solar-Powered Multi-Stage Desalination and Purification System

Team: Team Surya Jal
Project Category: Renewable Energy
OSFIC Problem Statement: PS-169

1. About the Project

Surya Jal is a proposed solar-powered desalination system designed to convert seawater into freshwater through evaporation, condensation and advanced purification.

2. Working Principle

Stage 1 – Solar Evaporation
Solar energy heats saline water, producing water vapour while leaving most dissolved salts behind.

Stage 2 – Condensation
Water vapour cools on an inclined surface and condenses into freshwater.

Stage 3 – Reverse Osmosis and Purification
The collected water undergoes further treatment using suitable filtration and RO technology.

Green Hydrogen Application – Electrolysis
After suitable water-quality testing and additional treatment if required, purified water can be used for electrolysis to produce hydrogen and oxygen. Electrolysis is an application, not a purification stage.

3. Key Innovations

Solar-powered operation.

Multi-stage water treatment.

Proposed heat recovery to improve efficiency.

Modular design for future development.

Potential application in coastal industries.

4. Project Objective

To explore a sustainable approach to freshwater sourcing for coastal communities and green hydrogen infrastructure.

5. Future Scope

Improve freshwater recovery.

Study energy efficiency.

Test water quality.

Develop an advanced heat-recovery system.

Explore larger-scale applications.
